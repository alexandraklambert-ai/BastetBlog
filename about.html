About

Bastet is a writing space for ideas around philosophy, politics and culture. I hope it is the kind of writing that will bring about a healthier kinder world.

I created this blog to help me file away the discussions I've had with myself and those around me. It is my contribution to a global conversation that is often talking about the wrong thing. Mainstream media, including social media, is clogging the global discourse. This blog aims not to do that.

I hope you find something here that is interesting, useful or inspiring. If you do, please share it with others. If you don't, please let me know how I can make it better.

Bastet

Bastet is a space for dissident ideas and radical kindness. It is internationalist, anti-capitalist, and strives to be at all times curious and optimistic.
